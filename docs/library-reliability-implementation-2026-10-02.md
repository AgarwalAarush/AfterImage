- The historical ten-minute model timeouts and disappeared interrupted runner remain execution failures with unresolved external causes. Added bounded process facts improve future diagnosis; deterministic tests do not establish why those calls stalled.
- Zero full kits passed the frozen model evaluation. No stored paper, figure or quiz was repaired during this task, and no historical verdict was rewritten. These shared-system tests do not certify generated content or independent scientific/visual acceptance.
