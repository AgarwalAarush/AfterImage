# Source-grounded subject mechanism walkthroughs

The optional mechanism sidecar teaches one paper-specific operation through stable symbolic objects and a finite storyboard. It complements quantitative controls by showing persistent collections, selected identities, available inputs, and changing output prefixes. It does not generate arbitrary SVG, styles, coordinates, scripts, measurements, or presumed numerical outputs.

`src/lib/subject-mechanism.ts` owns the bounded semantic schema and structural checks. Scenes contain five to eight stable objects using module, vector, tokens, or bank vocabulary; source/claim references; symbolic entities; and three to six complete snapshots. Each snapshot distinguishes pending, active, and retained objects. Pending objects cannot contain computed data. Transfers preserve identities at both endpoints, references must exist, and at least one data-bearing object must change. A diagram consisting only of highlighted computation boxes cannot pass. Labels must be complete compact symbols, with full meanings in narration rather than truncated mathematical expressions or control-token spellings.

`layoutSubjectMechanism` owns geometry and reuses the existing obstacle-aware routing from `scene-layout.ts`, without changing stored paper renderers. The new React component draws semantic identities in the product palette and uses the same storyboard for its static and animated states. Previous/Next and direct step controls pause playback. Autoplay starts while visible, pauses offscreen and on page hide, preserves its elapsed position, and stops at the final invariant. Reduced motion starts on the complete poster; explicit playback remains available. Narration remains readable Markdown with the subject reader's KaTeX handling.

Symbol pills reserve variable widths for complete 13px monospace labels and their padding. Object columns reflow before their data regions become narrower than 280px. Structural validation checks all beat glyphs at desktop and compatibility widths and rejects labels that cannot fit, instead of truncating mathematical identities or shrinking text.

The October 1 layout refinement sizes each stable object from its wrapped title/detail and maximum entity count across the full storyboard. Text-only modules reserve no empty glyph strip. A module with one complete symbol uses a compact inline badge when the title and symbol fit together; modules with multiple symbols keep a full glyph row. Whisper's acoustic encoder now stays 60px tall across all five beats, including its initial input symbol, while its multi-input decoder retains the space required for all five entities. Heights and positions do not change when playback advances. Each row uses its tallest object and vertically centers smaller neighbors, allowing horizontal connections to share a center line.

Aligned endpoints use a direct center-to-center boundary segment when no intervening object or existing parallel connector occupies the path. Longer dependencies, diagonal relationships and feedback retain the reviewed obstacle-aware routes. This removes the narrow-column doglegs between Whisper's acoustic input and encoder and between emitted output and retained prefix. Routing and typography remain owned by Subjects; `scene-layout.ts`, historical paper diagrams and semantic JSON are unchanged. Renderer fingerprints must be rebound and visual reviews repeated before publishing affected sidecars.

Regression tests cover centered vertical and horizontal connections, obstructed long links, clear object interiors, per-object content heights, inline single-symbol modules, and stable geometry after reversing storyboard order. Browser review at the actual reader column width remains required.

`scripts/generate-subject-mechanisms.ts` uses the reviewed lesson's unchanged private claim ledger and digest-matched primary excerpts. Structural checks and complete math validation precede an independent source, pedagogy, and temporal-state review. DQN scenes must respect the source version rather than importing later target-network or replay variants; Whisper scenes must distinguish acoustic representations, task/language/timestamp conditioning, and emitted prefixes. Drafts, exact excerpts, diagnostics, and review reports remain ignored private artifacts. Four targeted repair attempts follow the initial scene. `--resume-drafts` retains prior semantic structure and actionable findings.

Source-passed sidecars are saved under `src/content/subjects/mechanisms/` with their parent lesson digest, semantic digest, and renderer fingerprint. The ordinary reader loader accepts only a visually approved, current sidecar. Development reviewers may explicitly inspect source-passed candidates, and production must never enable that option. A fingerprint-bound visual review with no findings is required for `--approve-visual <identity> --review-file <report>`. Parent or renderer changes invalidate approval. Source correctness, automatic routing checks, and visual/pedagogical acceptance remain separate gates.

Source and editorial reviews require every check to report `passed: true` and an empty findings array. A contradictory passing flag cannot suppress a finding, and a failed flag without findings still fails closed. Regression coverage checks both cases. The saved independent review candidates for DQN and Whisper were rechecked against their exact current semantic scene digests after this stricter gate; all checks had zero findings.

Run `node --import tsx scripts/generate-subject-mechanisms.ts --id <identity[,identity]>` for named lessons or `--all` for the currently reviewed lesson set. Concurrency defaults to one and is bounded to three. The runtime loader hashes the four files named by `subjectMechanismRendererFiles`; release file tracing must include those files and the mechanism sidecar JSON. No library snapshots, owner SQLite data, storage bridge actions, historical paper scenes, or worker behavior change.

Each paper captures its current renderer fingerprint at generation start and rejects changes during its review. An explicit `--resume-drafts --use-authored-labels` retry may load that identity's private `authored-labels.json` to repair whitelisted prose/symbol strings while preserving semantic identities, states, and relationships. The complete source/state/math/geometry review still runs. Prose targets leave headroom below schema limits, and a clipped takeaway or unclosed equation receives a targeted label/prose repair rather than silently publishing incomplete text.

Explicit authored prose is reapplied after each candidate repair and remains subject to the complete independent review. Resumption selects a complete JSON scene, skips incomplete transport outputs, and saves `resumed-candidate.json` before invoking any model stage that could reuse its output filename. This preserves the previous semantic candidate if a resumed transport is interrupted or returns an empty response.

## Local authored candidates

`generate-subject-mechanisms.ts --id <id> --candidate-file .artifacts/subjects/<candidate>.json` begins with a complete locally authored scene. It validates source/claim references, math, labels, routes, and state structure against the current reviewed parent before requesting independent full science/teaching/state review. The candidate remains private until that review passes; browser visual acceptance remains a separate required gate. This supports deliberate repair of dependencies that need multiple explanatory beats instead of increasing simultaneous-edge bounds.

The authored T5 scene uses five ordered example tokens, removes one adjacent two-token span, and shows the exact input/target transformation: `red birds sing near home` becomes input `red S0 near home` and target `S0 birds sing S1`. Sentinel meanings and target order are explicit. Its companion lesson and quiz passed all four review gates before the unrelated image-patch control was removed; attention remains as a separate prerequisite control. The source-passed scene was rebound only after checking that the parent claim ledger and source bindings remained unchanged, with visual approval reset.

The authored DiffusionBlocks scene separates two information regimes. Training constructs `zb = y + σb ε` directly and optimizes the selected block against the available clean target. Inference makes that target unavailable, retains one sampler object through `z0 → z1 → z2`, and selects high- and lower-noise specialists for successive updates. Symbolic denoising estimates describe dependencies without fabricating learned numerical predictions. Both scenes passed independent science, teaching and temporal-state review plus host math/relationship checks and bounds at 760, 700, 560 and 380 pixels. The renderer files remain unchanged; current desktop visual acceptance is required before ordinary-reader visibility.

This includes the reduced top inset and renewed approvals after the final priority prose changes. AlphaZero's final authored scene passed its first independent review after simplifying the explanation to the primary text's supported dependencies. It shows network prior guidance, per-action visit/value information during search, nonterminal leaf evaluation, completed root visits, and sampling from the resulting self-play distribution. It does not assert a backup implementation or persistent leaf-record store. Earlier failed candidates were retained privately rather than published.
