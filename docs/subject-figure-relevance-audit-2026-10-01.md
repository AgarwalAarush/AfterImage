# Quantitative figure relevance audit

This bounded audit inspected the original lesson JSON and exact implemented numerical contracts while the 100-topic batch was still running. Its initial availability snapshot contained 74 reviewed lessons; all 74 matched their public review/source fingerprints and substantive primary-source contexts. Three topics were active and 23 pending, with no finished failure available for independent recovery. It did not edit lessons or mechanism sidecars.

The scientific gates establish truthful explanations and contract fidelity, but they do not by themselves ensure that every numerical control teaches the selected paper's distinctive method. The first 74 lessons contain 180 numerical figures; 69 use the same scalar optimizer, four-score attention, or single-vector normalization primitives. Those can teach useful prerequisites. Several placements instead require long explanations of why their control differs from the actual method, and would serve the learner better as direct method examples.

## Explicit control-removal review

A subsequent complete-catalog audit selected nineteen controls whose implemented operations did not explain the paper's contribution. The editorial correction removes the exact figure and its section anchor, retains at least one authored prerequisite control in every lesson, and replaces only the affected section's prose with the actual method. It also corrects acquisition narration in the selected Seq2Seq and DiT conclusion sections.
