## Extraction and evidence boundaries

HTML extraction now includes appendices, inert listings and preformatted code. Embedded `data:text/plain;base64` listings may be decoded as bounded UTF-8 text; no code, assets, links or document instructions execute. Captions, listing children and nested scientific sections are deduplicated. Main-section IDs retain their previous numbering; appendix IDs derive from anchors, with citable continuation suffixes. Code indentation/operators are preserved and long listings split into bounded chunks.

The private `ResearchBundle` holds a catalogue of at most 128 chunks, an active context of at most 14 excerpts, and section/block coverage with explicit omissions and truncation. Every chunk is at most 9,500 characters including disclosure markers. Initial selection gives each section a first-chunk opportunity before continuations. A resolved arXiv version is retained for source links and same-version PDF lookup. Full-text means evidence beyond the abstract, not complete coverage. Missing structural/listing data, catalogue omissions or truncated blocks trigger bounded PDF fallback; the existing PDF page/byte/time bounds remain and PDF omissions are recorded.

Public `Source`, `Paper` and API/export shapes are unchanged. Public extraction projects only scope and excerpts. Manifests, catalogues, adjudication receipts and raw model diagnostics never enter browser/library state. Owner-uploaded documents are outside this workflow.

## Reconciliation and repair authority

Technical and rendered reviews inspect the same candidate. Review output schemas allow only exact native target paths and current active excerpt citation IDs; passage IDs cannot masquerade as source IDs. Citation enums refresh after enrichment. Scientific findings are adjudicated as supported defect, unsupported review demand, insufficient evidence or unresolved source conflict, regardless of their content/representation ownership. Models select IDs from received passages; the worker materializes exact original text and derives its citation IDs, avoiding redundant model-authored citation lists. This avoids requiring the model to faithfully transcribe LaTeX or code. Empty, invented, inexact or stale evidence cannot authorize a scientific edit. Every supported blocker records its essential plan requirement/scientific invariant and a fingerprint of active source content.

Each opening or supplement permits two evidence-enrichment passes, each selecting at most four omitted same-paper catalogue chunks. Candidate, plan and open-finding citations are pinned. Unknown selections, duplicate IDs or required evidence exceeding the 14-slot limit fail privately. Lookup never accepts a model-provided URL. After enrichment, citation schemas and source/review bindings refresh, prior verdicts are invalidated, and complete review repeats with unchanged content. Open findings survive refreshes and omissions remain disputed.

A source-proven unsupported demand is recorded as a private candidate/binding-bound adjudication receipt. It requires a fresh full review and explicit adjudication verification; a still-blocking fresh finding, stale receipt or ordinary omission cannot close it. Four review-refresh cycles per candidate bound this orchestration independently of the two stage-wide enrichment passes. A genuine defect still requires ordinary correction and verification.

Scientific deterministic-validation findings follow the same reconciliation before editing, including still-open obligations after source refresh. The locator cannot supply its own source-proof object. It must identify native validation dependencies, including parent figure provenance when making a panel illustrative. Numeric source findings remain scientific when reviewers use underscore category aliases or quantitative wording. Rejected-proposal bookkeeping does not hide repeated repairs from the existing single fallback; failed proposals still consume attempts.

Mathematical coverage is assessed against the mechanism plan during the first review. Later expansion requests must cite a missed essential requirement or concrete scientific defect; optional elaboration remains a suggestion. Changed equations, explanations and examples (including removal) receive a source/edge-case audit before adoption. Supplement proposals audit figure quantities and answer/distractor dependencies. Unsupported proposals retain the prior candidate and consume the existing attempt. Invalid replacement plans likewise cannot be adopted. Four opening/two supplement edits and one shared representation fallback remain unchanged; execution failures stop separately.

Review bindings include extraction, bundle, PDF, reconciliation and patch-audit implementations alongside sources, schemas, plan, renderer, fonts, engines and rubric. Opening version is `evidence-repair-v5`; supplement version is `evidence-study-repair-v4`. Private technical/visual records include binding suffixes so refreshes retain separate evidence files.
