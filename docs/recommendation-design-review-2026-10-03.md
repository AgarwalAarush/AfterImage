# Recommendation interface refinement

This refines the recommendation-learning draft on `codex/recommendation-learning`. It preserves AfterImage’s existing DM Sans, Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono assets, theme tokens, reader geometry and generation boundaries. No dependencies, worker code, storage transport, Library content or production state change in this refinement.

## Review and resulting behavior

The review applied Impeccable’s audit/critique/polish lenses, UX Analysis, Emil’s interaction and motion guidance, Quiet Tech Design, Apple’s feedback/agency/typography principles, and the relevant editorial and existing-site design checks. Existing product identity takes precedence over conflicting marketing templates or stylistic rebrands. Independent source/screenshot assessments examined usability and technical findings separately.

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-width interests panel with distant link-like choices | Compact sidebar alongside the research form, with native segmented radios | Group each topic with its choices; one Tab stop per interest |
| Preferences silently saved while direction remained a draft | Automatic/pending/failed preference status and explicit saved/unsaved direction status | Explain the two saving models without adding another save button |
| Cards showed abstract text but omitted recommendation explanations | Grounded reason and a visible “Read for” focus | Let readers assess the suggestion before opening it |
| Feedback effects hidden behind bare labels | Effect hints and a labeled reader feedback control | Distinguish dislike, prerequisites, known papers and Library saves |
| Menu closure or card removal could lose focus | Escape/selection restore the trigger; consumption focuses a remaining card or Next reads | Keep keyboard navigation in context |
| Undo timed out and could be overwritten by unrelated notifications | Persistent actionable dismissal notification, separately from transient status/error messages | Keep recovery reachable without stealing next-card focus |
| Pending direction save could label newer edits saved | Revision-bound completion retains newer unsaved edits and does not navigate away from them | Avoid hiding work that was not submitted |
| Refill used an amber notice | Quiet inline status | Treat discovery as ordinary work rather than a warning |

Shared control styling remains in `src/app/ui.css`. Reader prose, quiz presentation and diagram geometry are unchanged. No decorative entrance or layout animation is added; keyboard actions are immediate. The new segmented radios and reading switch use the established appearance-control vocabulary and visible focus. Selected strengths have a semantic accent indicator in both themes.

- Exact viewport, computed-font/contrast and every-state Subjects presentation acceptance are not established by these native screenshots. Shared Dia control was also changing windows during later interaction checks; uncompleted checks are not reported as passed.
