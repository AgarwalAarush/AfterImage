# Dynamic reading interests

Reading interests can now grow beyond the original four topics. The panel leads with Suggested interests: an independently reviewed explanation, an expandable list of supporting papers, and Add / Ignore. Manual Add accepts arbitrary research topics. Remove retires a topic while retaining private choice history and suppression; manual re-add restores its identity. Explicit strengths remain under the reader's control.

## Inference and evidence

`src/lib/interest-suggestions.ts` owns durable decisions, evidence selection and publication. `worker/interest-discovery.ts` proposes topics without a fixed taxonomy, then performs a separate structured semantic review using the existing model launcher. A result must describe a substantive recurring topic supported by canonical titles/abstracts from multiple distinct positively engaged papers. Source excerpts alone cannot certify a fabricated theme or alias. Useful subtopics may emerge beneath broad interests; synonyms and near-identical restatements are suppressed.

A topic needs at least two sources with confirmed Save / Prepare / Reading / Useful evidence. Mixed or passive-only topics need at least four sources and total decayed signal of 1.5. Sources below 0.1 are omitted; latest Not for me / Already know it / Too advanced feedback removes positive evidence as defined by the existing preference policy. Passive evidence respects the reading opt-out. Discovery never treats metadata presence, quick opens or external reading as engagement.

The model receives at most 60 canonical source records: up to 30 from recent positive activity, then the strongest remaining evidence. This keeps older Useful papers from crowding out changing tastes. Repeated passages from the same paper normalize to one canonical excerpt; they cannot count as additional independent sources. A second model call reviews topic meaning, aliases, explanation, existing/Off/ignored overlaps, and exact source identities/digests. Unsupported primary topics, reasons or evidence are omitted. An otherwise independently approved topic may survive with all optional aliases removed when their separate support verdict fails; the private receipt records the dropped aliases. There are at most two model calls per check and at most three visible suggestions.

Add adopts the topic at Normal strength. Ignore suppresses the topic and its aliases without creating a paper dislike. Remove similarly retires an explicit interest without erasing reading/feedback history. Manual re-add can reverse topic suppression. Up to 24 interests may be retained in the editable panel; Remove makes space as interests change. All these actions and explicit retries have durable event IDs; replay cannot schedule more work.

## Background work and compatibility

An additive `interests` job runs independently of Next reads vacancies. Confirmed explicit choices can queue it. Engagement writes remain small acknowledgements and do not queue discovery or generation. Compatible workers reconcile new passive evidence during their existing idle heartbeat/claim cadence. Page loads, hover and prefetch start no work.

Scheduling coalesces one active interest job, remembers the evidence fingerprints actually claimed beyond the rolling job list, limits checks to two per hour within the existing global job bound, and permits only one immediate stale-result follow-up. Unchanged failures do not retry automatically. Explicit retry requires a new action ID and respects the cooldown/budget. Disabling learning or withdrawing the required evidence cancels queued inference before model inputs are claimed.

Workers advertise `dynamic-interests-v1`. Older workers cannot claim `interests` jobs, or recommendation jobs after arbitrary interests have been added. Interest claims carry bounded source inputs, not the entire library/ledger. Completion rechecks the profile revision and evidence fingerprint before publishing. Learning remains disabled by default for release staging.

Accepted interests drive their own established/recent searches. Six search lanes rotate across the entire active vocabulary and scout queries rather than permanently truncating later topics. Broad labels can match semantically through canonical excerpts plus an independent interest/disabled-topic review. Private recommendation assignments ensure turning Off or removing a topic also removes existing nonliteral matches.

State schema version 2 and signed storage transport remain unchanged. The browser receives labels, editable strengths, reviewed explanations, supporting paper identities/titles and coarse discovery status. Private quotes, signals, aliases, event history, ignored-topic history, evidence fingerprints, search cursors, assignments and review receipts stay out of normal state and exports. Existing safe job projections retain accurate polling and preparation queue positions. Recommendation jobs run before short interest checks, then reading-kit preparation; active work is not interrupted.

## Verification and release

Automated regressions cover arbitrary topic creation, restoration/removal, aliases and duplicates, ignored/Off suppression, canonical evidence, passive thresholds, changing tastes, durable replay protection, failure bounds, stale publication, private exports, older worker compatibility, disabled-policy cancellation and nonliteral-topic exclusions. `scripts/evaluate-interests.ts --run` runs six bounded synthetic public-fixture model trials without owner state, cookies, environment files or storage access. Its private receipts are kept under ignored `.artifacts/interest-discovery-evaluation/`.

Native Dia checks cover Suggested interest Add/Ignore, evidence disclosure, arbitrary manual entry, duplicate guidance, Remove, native arrow-key strength changes and persistence across reload in Light/Dark. The localhost fixture is synthetic and runs no generation worker. Detailed captures remain private. These checks do not renew the separate every-beat Subjects visual acceptance.

Release remains held by the existing Subjects presentation gate. Deploy compatible web/API first, then update the discovery worker after active work finishes while preserving newer production generation fixes. Enable learning only after compatibility and live smoke checks. Rollback disables the learning policy while retaining recorded choices and suppressions. Do not deploy an older whole worker checkout over newer generation/rendering fixes.
